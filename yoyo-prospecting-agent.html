PHASE 1 OF 5

Discover

STEP 01Implemented prototype · proposed validation

Problem Definition

Sales teams must research an account, decide whether it is relevant, identify a supported business need, and write a credible first message. Manual research is inconsistent, while automated outreach can produce generic copy or unsupported claims. The product opportunity is to reduce research effort while preserving relevance, factual support, and reviewer control.

Product objective: Help sales users prepare supported, relevant outreach with less manual research and a clear approval trail.

STEP 02Workflow assumptions · user research proposed

User Empathy and Discovery

The sales representative needs a concise research brief and an editable draft. The sales manager needs consistent qualification and a review trail. Both need the product to distinguish a verified account signal from a role-based hypothesis.

  • Map the current account-research and message-review workflow with sales users.
  • Compare generic drafting with evidence-led drafting on the same prospect set.
  • Validate qualification criteria, reviewer workload, and acceptable research cost before testing outbound engagement.

No completed customer interviews, survey results or testimonials are claimed.

STEP 03Product decision rationale

Why AI Was Appropriate

Web research collects account evidence. Specialist model calls select an outreach angle and draft a message from a bounded brief. Deterministic checks enforce identity, approved capabilities, evidence linkage, and review state. LangGraph coordinates the workflow and pauses for a human decision.

Alternative considered

Use a rules-based eligibility check and a fixed template when context is limited. Reserve model calls for research synthesis and account-specific drafting. Do not add model agents for exact validation or database updates.

Bound the model’s role; use deterministic checks for eligibility, explicit constraints and state integrity. Human accountability remains part of the product.

STEP 07Product requirements · artifact available

AI PRD

Users: Sales development representatives, account researchers, and sales managers.

Requirements: Retain evidence provenance; hold company/title conflicts; restrict product claims to approved wording; isolate prospect state; validate edited drafts; prevent duplicate review events.

Failure handling: Research failure → bounded role-led fallback or hold. Unsupported claims → correction, then hold if unresolved. Stale review state → suppress action controls and require a fresh record.

Non-functional requirements — proposed: access boundaries, auditable versions, bounded retries, task-specific latency/cost budgets and accountable support.

Dependencies: authorised prospect input, accurate capability definitions, supported research/model access, persistent review state and reviewer capacity.

Design & Plan

STEP 09Source-reviewed implementation

Solution Architecture

Verified implementation: Streamlit UI, Python validation, OpenAI Responses SDK with hosted web search, LangGraph state transitions and interrupts, and SQLite persistence. Tavily, Firecrawl, numerical ICP scoring, and automatic message sending are not implemented in this version.

Architecture choices and constraints.
Trade-offProduct decision
Evidence-led vs. role-led draftingUse verified company evidence when available. If research fails, permit a bounded role-led question or hold the prospect; never invent an account event.
Agent autonomy vs. reviewer controlGenerate a draft, validate it, and pause for Approve, Edit, or Reject. No sending capability exists.
Flexible research vs. bounded costThe normal path has three specialist model calls. Follow-up research and writer corrections are bounded; exact validation runs in Python.
Throughput vs. review integrityProcess prospects independently and persist identifiers. Resume the correct review thread and reject stale or mismatched records.

LangGraph orchestrates workflow state and pauses for review; SQLite persists draft and review state. LangSmith could support future tracing and evaluation but is not claimed as an integration. Tracing explains execution; evaluation judges it against a standard.

STEP 12Executed regression subset · model quality unmeasured

Offline Evaluation

Executed checks

The full offline suite passed 158 tests in 37.71 seconds; 11 opt-in live-provider tests were skipped. Coverage includes agent contracts, persistence, review controls, Streamlit UI and startup, and claim boundaries. This is software regression evidence, not model-quality accuracy.

Dataset and boundaries

Tests use synthetic prospect fixtures, API-shaped doubles, temporary SQLite databases, and real LangGraph checkpoints. No real outreach was sent.

Release-blocking failures

Unsupported product expansion, wrong-prospect review, unlinked evidence, duplicate events, or synthetic contacts entering real outcome metrics.

Model-quality evaluation

Create a separate reviewer-labeled dataset for qualification and message relevance. Historical documents describe synthetic live smoke checks; no live API check was re-run for this portfolio update.

STEP 13Local prototype · public launch proposed

Go-Live Criteria

Current state: a local prototype with source-reviewed workflow and focused offline regression evidence. Proposed next steps: representative live-model evaluation, staff-only review pilot, then a controlled manual-outreach experiment. It is not a production SaaS deployment.

Require valid evidence boundaries, isolated prospect state, reliable review actions, and no unresolved critical regressions. Pilot approval also requires acceptable reviewer-labeled relevance, cost, and manual-review effort.

Assign product and engineering owners; test access controls, review capacity, dependency failures and support before a pilot. Critical failures block release.

Rollback: Pause new jobs on provider or integrity failures. Preserve persisted drafts and review records. Return to manual research and drafting; do not automatically replay interrupted paid calls.

Monitor & Improve

STEP 14Proposed monitoring · not production results

Online Evaluation

Reviewer workflow

Track unchanged approval, edits, rejection reasons, review time, and repeated use in a limited internal pilot.

Commercial experiment

Only after manual outreach is authorized, compare evidence-led and baseline messages with predeclared segmentation and sample-size requirements.

Correct denominators

Measure positive replies and qualified meetings per distinct manually contacted prospect; exclude synthetic rows.

Quality and trust

Audit factual support and generic or duplicate copy; track opt-outs, complaints, dependency failures, and cost.

Use privacy-conscious traces with access controls, redaction and retention limits. Monitor task quality, latency, cost and changed input patterns separately. LangSmith is an optional proposed improvement.

STEP 15Unmeasured outcomes · pilot plan

Business Outcome Monitoring

Implemented CSV-to-human-review workflow with persistent state and bounded model/tool stages. The full offline suite passed 158 tests; 11 opt-in live-provider tests were skipped. There is no measured reply-rate, meeting-conversion, or customer-efficiency improvement.

In a future authorised manual-outreach pilot, track positive replies, qualified meetings, opt-outs and complaints using distinct contacted prospects as denominators. Exclude synthetic records.

Compare equivalent cohorts, record the baseline and measurement window, and predeclare experimental allocation and sample-size needs before making causal claims.

STEP 16Feedback-loop design

Learning and Iteration

Reliability depends on evidence boundaries and review-state integrity as much as message quality. The next product decision is whether the workflow saves reviewer time while improving qualified engagement.

Production signal → failure analysis → root cause → requirement or test → backlog item → offline regression → release decision → monitoring.

Illustrative priority: an unsupported account claim becomes a failure category, a validation requirement and a new evidence-regression case before expanding outreach automation.

Prioritize reviewer-labeled prospect suitability and draft relevance. Add numerical ICP scoring only after eligibility criteria are stable and validated.
