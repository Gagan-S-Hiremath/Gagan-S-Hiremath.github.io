01Problem & opportunity

SLA analysts manually searched contracts, interpreted KPI tables, and consolidated reporting. Different layouts, scanned pages, merged cells, and repeated attributes made the workflow slow and error-prone. Operations teams lacked a consistent view of their service commitments.

Customer objective

Make approved SLA commitments accessible as structured data and source-grounded answers.

User needs

The analyst needs accurate extraction with clause-level traceability. The operations lead needs approved KPIs without opening every contract. Both need a clear review path when documents contain conflicting thresholds.

Operations lead / SLA analyst

Priorities: accurate commitments, clause-level traceability, and consistent reporting.

Workflow-informed user needs

02Discovery & product judgment

Why AI is appropriate

OCR identifies text and document layout. An LLM maps different contractual terms to a standard KPI schema. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) provides answers from approved records and relevant clauses. Schema validation and human review control what reaches reporting.

Alternative considered

Use template-based parsing for consistent document layouts. Use AI where terminology and structure vary. Keep manual validation for ambiguous clauses and unreadable scans.

Discovery and validation priorities

  • Map document arrival, extraction, validation, reporting, and query workflows.
  • Interview analysts and operations leads about review effort, exceptions, and trust.
  • Baseline processing time and retrieval time separately, using comparable documents and tasks.
Prioritization rationale
Prioritize a reliable extraction-and-approval path before expanding document families or conversational access.

03Product requirements & MVP

MVP scope

One representative document family first; 15 core KPI definitions; evidence links; validation queue; reporting export; grounded queries.

Functional requirements

Preserve source references; version schemas; detect missing required fields; record reviewer decisions; prevent unapproved records entering reporting.

Non-goals

Contract interpretation as legal advice, autonomous contract changes, and automatic publication of unresolved values.

Non-functional requirements

Define latency and total-task cost budgets, versioned configuration, access boundaries, failure recovery, and auditability before a pilot.

04Architecture & AI workflow

The documented workflow uses SharePoint and Power Automate for intake, Azure AI Document Intelligence for OCR and layout, Azure OpenAI for semantic mapping, and SQL for validation state. Data Factory and Databricks support downstream data integration; Power BI provides reporting. Human approval precedes publication.

Trade-offProduct decision
Flexible schema vs. reliable reportingUse a versioned canonical KPI model, retaining additional attributes separately to contain schema expansion.
Automation vs. accountabilityPropose extracted values with evidence; require review for ambiguity and preserve validation status.
Fast answers vs. grounded answersRetrieve approved records and source passages; decline answers without sufficient evidence.

Failure handling

Unreadable scans → request a clearer copy. Conflicting values → show both sources and require review. Missing evidence → do not invent a value.

Observability & data boundaries

Record input and version identifiers, validation status, corrections, tool failures, latency, and cost. Restrict data access, redact traces, and define retention before collecting pilot data.

Offline evaluation

Dataset

Proposed 50-case development set: 30 typical, 10 edge, 10 adversarial; separate 50-case held-out set. Split by base document to avoid near-duplicate leakage.

Gold labels

An analyst labels fields and supporting evidence; a second reviewer resolves disagreements.

Checks

Exact field match, source entailment, table-context mapping, required-field completeness, injection resistance.

Analysis

Compare prompt versions by document family, merged cells, repeated keys, scan quality, and severity. List every regression.

Online evaluation & experimentation

Quality

Sample approved records for source-backed correctness; track reviewer correction rate and escaped errors.

User value

Observe completion time for processing and retrieval as separate workflows.

Operations

Monitor queue age, human-review workload, p50/p95 latency, failures, and cost per accepted document.

Feedback loop

Turn corrected and failed documents into new regression cases; re-run after prompt, model, or schema changes.

Predeclare the primary outcome, guardrails, allocation unit, and sample-size requirement before an A/B test. Small usability studies provide directional feedback, not causal business uplift.

Release gate
Proposed launch gate: required-field quality meets the predeclared target, unresolved conflicts cannot publish, and no critical leakage or instruction-following failures occur in the qualification suite. A zero count in a small test set is not proof of zero production risk.

06Launch & iteration

Current state and release sequence

Proposed sequence: offline qualification → internal analyst review → shadow processing → a limited document-family pilot → gradual expansion. No historical rollout evidence is claimed here.

Operational readiness

Version models, prompts, tool contracts, and configuration. Assign product and engineering owners, test access permissions and dependency outages, and verify support and rollback before a pilot.

Rollback and escalation

Pause automated publication on a critical evidence failure or data exposure. Revert to manual validation, retain queued documents, and roll back the prompt/schema version. Name an operations owner and on-call engineering owner before a pilot.

Next validation priority

Prioritize document families by ambiguity and review burden. Measure extraction quality and reviewer effort together before expanding automated publication.

07Outcomes & learning

The professional solution connected document parsing, KPI extraction, human validation, dashboards, and conversational access. It improved access to SLA information and reduced manual processing and retrieval effort. Quantified impact is not presented without measurement context.

Product learning and next decision

Schema flexibility creates reporting complexity. The next product decision is which KPI fields belong in the canonical schema and which should remain contextual attributes. Source traceability and approval state are essential to that decision.
